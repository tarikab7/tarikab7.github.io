Privacy Policy

Last Updated: May 31, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how we collect, use, process, and protect your information when you download, install, and use the mobile application "Multi-Minigames: 1v1 Offline Battles" ("the App").

👤 1. Data Controller

The Data Controller responsible for the processing of your personal information in connection with the App is:

  • Developer: Seyhan Tarik Akgöz
  • Contact Email: studio@tarikakgoz.com
  • Developer Profile: tarikab7 on GitHub

📡 2. Information Collection and Processing

The App is designed primarily as a local, offline peer-to-peer (P2P) multiplayer gaming hub. We are committed to minimizing data collection to respect your privacy.

A. Data Processed Locally (P2P Offline Infrastructure)

  • Local Microphone (RECORD_AUDIO): Used to record and send voice messages during local P2P matches. Audio recordings are encoded on-device and sent directly to your opponent. We do not store or transmit voice data to external servers.
  • Nearby Connections API: Used to discover and connect to nearby devices via Bluetooth/Wi-Fi. Connection data is kept in memory on-device and never stored externally.

B. Third-Party Services (Advertising & Diagnostics)

  • Google AdMob: May process device-level ad identifiers (Android Advertising ID) for ad analytics and targeting. See app-ads.txt verification.
  • Google Firebase (Analytics & Crashlytics): Collects anonymous crash telemetry and aggregated performance statistics to improve game stability.
  • Consent Management (UMP SDK): Presenting GDPR consent panels for users in the EEA & UK.

⚖️ 3. Legal Bases for Processing (GDPR)

We process personal data under Consent (Art. 6(1)(a)), Performance of a Contract (Art. 6(1)(b)), and Legitimate Interests (Art. 6(1)(f)).

👶 4. Children's Privacy

The App is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children. Ad SDKs are configured to restrict personalized data collection for users under the age of consent.

📬 5. Contact Us

If you have any questions or concerns regarding your privacy, please reach out to us at: studio@tarikakgoz.com
