Privacy Policy for Multi-Minigames: 1v1 Offline Battles

Last Updated: May 31, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how we collect, use, process, and protect your information when you download, install, and use the mobile application "Multi-Minigames: 1v1 Offline Battles" ("the App").

1. Data Controller

The Data Controller responsible for the processing of your personal information in connection with the App is:

  • Developer: Seyhan Tarik Akgöz
  • Contact Email: studio@tarikakgoz.com

2. Information Collection and Processing

The App is designed primarily as a local, offline peer-to-peer (P2P) multiplayer gaming hub. We are committed to minimizing data collection to respect your privacy.

A. Data Processed Locally (P2P Offline Infrastructure)

  • Local Microphone (RECORD_AUDIO): The App uses your device's microphone to allow recording and sending voice messages to your opponent during local P2P matches. Audio recordings are encoded and transmitted directly to the other player via P2P. We do not store or transmit your voice data to external servers.
  • Nearby Connections API: To discover and connect to nearby devices, the App uses Google's Nearby Connections API. Connection info and player names are handled in memory on-device and are never stored externally.

B. Data Processed by Third-Party Services (Advertising & Diagnostics)

  • Google AdMob: AdMob may collect and process device-level identifiers (such as Advertising ID) for ad analytics and targeting.
  • Google Firebase (Analytics & Crashlytics): Collects anonymous crash reports and aggregated usage statistics to improve stability.
  • Consent Management (UMP SDK): Presenting consent dialogs for users in EEA/UK under GDPR.

3. Legal Bases for Processing (GDPR Compliance)

We process personal data under Consent (Art. 6(1)(a)), Performance of a Contract (Art. 6(1)(b)), and Legitimate Interests (Art. 6(1)(f)).

4. Children's Privacy

The App is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children.

5. Contact Us

If you have any questions or concerns, please reach out to us at: studio@tarikakgoz.com
